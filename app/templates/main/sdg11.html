How CivicPulse Drives SDG 11 Targets

Civic technology transforms passive city dwellers into active co-custodians of urban public goods.

Target 11.1
Safe & Resilient Basic Urban Services

By 2030, ensure access for all to adequate, safe, and affordable housing and basic services, including reliable water supply and road infrastructure.

  • Roads & Infrastructure: Pothole detection prevents traffic collisions and pedestrian injuries.
  • Water Supply: Rapid reporting of pipeline bursts curtails clean water wastage and prevents localized flooding.
  • Electrical Lighting: Restoring broken streetlights enhances safety for women and commuters at night.
Target 11.6
Environmental Impact & Municipal Waste

By 2030, reduce the adverse per capita environmental impact of cities, including by paying special attention to air quality and municipal waste management.

  • Solid Waste Management: Elimination of overflowing waste dump spots stops leachate from contaminating soil and water tables.
  • Vector Control: Eradication of stagnant water bodies directly suppresses dengue and malaria outbreaks.
  • Clean Stormwater Channels: Unclogging drains stops urban street runoff blockages during monsoon rainfalls.
