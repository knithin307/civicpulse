How CivicPulse Works

Empowering direct civic communication with end-to-end transparency and zero bureaucratic runarounds.

1. Pin & Document

Citizens spot a hazard (pothole, burst pipe, dark streetlight), pinpoint exact GPS coordinates on our Leaflet map, and snap a photo.

2. Triage & Field Dispatch

Automated SLA timers route tickets directly to the responsible municipal department (Roads, Waste, Power, Water). Work crews acknowledge tickets and log updates.

3. Photo Proof & Citizen Audit

Officers upload After Photo evidence to close tickets. Reporting citizens review the repair, submit star ratings, and confirm community safety.
